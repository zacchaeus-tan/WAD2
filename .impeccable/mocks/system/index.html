The Blaze Means Stop Rule. Blaze marks a failed check, a rating, or the one key action on a screen. It is never decoration and never a second button colour.

The Shape Beside Colour Rule. Heat colour never stands alone: every grade shows its shape and name, and every pass or fail shows a tick or cross in a box. The Two Heats Rule. Bright heat only sits on Night or ink; on ground or white, use the heat-ink step.

The Signed Climb Rule. Climb is always signed (+893 m); highest point never is (3,115 m). Metric only. The No Eyebrow Rule. Small uppercase labels name a value or a control, never sit above a heading.

Shape & depth

Square everywhere. Edges come from weight, not curves. Shadows only for things that really float.

2px ink rule

Opens and closes a section, outlines buttons and switches.

1px hairlines

Between rows in lists, numbers, the register and the fit check.

Float shadow

Dropdown panels and stops over the trail: 0 12px 32px / 18%.

Round is natural

Only the trail, grass, the hiker marker and dial arc are round.

Motion

One curve for everything. Content is visible by default; motion only adds to it, and reduced motion shows the end state.

Expo ease-outcubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Every transition: hovers at 0.2s, reveals at 0.8s, needles at 1.6s.

Draw in

Bars, route lines and the trail fill along their length as they enter view. Hover or focus this card to play it.

Rise

Sections lift 28px into place once. The grade needles swing from Easy to their value, with the community needle 0.4s behind.

prefers-reduced-motion → end state

Motion components

Four small pieces built with Motion. Each plays once when it comes into view; the buttons replay them here.

Number ticker

Big Montserrat figures count up from zero once, over 1.2s on the expo curve. Tabular figures keep the width steady. The real value is in the page, so screen readers and reduced motion get it straight away.

Do and don't

Do

  • Keep every corner square and draw edges with 2px ink rules or 1px hairlines.
  • Pair every heat colour with the grade's shape and name.
  • Set compared numbers in Montserrat tabular figures, metric, climb signed.
  • Use pine for bars and controls you act on.
  • Dim route photos to about 45% over Night so the line reads first.

Don't

  • Use rounded pills, rounded cards or soft panels.
  • Add shadows to flat content at rest.
  • Put a small uppercase kicker above a heading.
  • Use blaze as decoration or a second button colour.
  • Draw ticks or crosses with CSS borders or Unicode.

Know before you go.
