Every summit starts with an honest answer.

CAIRN is a trek planner for hikers in Southeast Asia. Take a short quiz about the walks you've done, and every route shows whether it suits you and where it will stretch you. Grades shift with real hiker reviews, and AI helps you plan the days and pack the right gear.

How it works

See your suitability for each route

Fixed rules check each route against your history. A route can ask up to 25% more than you've done before and still count as within your experience.

  • ComfortableWithin what you have done before.
  • Good challengeA step up in one or two areas.
  • A stretchWell beyond what you have done so far.

The grade moves when hikers say so.

Every route keeps its official grade next to a community grade from reviews. When hikers rate it harder, the grade shifts, and the fit check uses it.
